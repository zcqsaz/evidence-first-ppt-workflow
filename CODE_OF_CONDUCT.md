# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or field of study.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and evidence-based;
- critique work without attacking people;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently;
- respect confidentiality, copyright and research ethics;
- do not upload private presentation content to public issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, plagiarism and knowingly falsified evidence are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be made through the private contact channel configured by the repository host.

This concise policy is inspired by Contributor Covenant 2.1. When a public maintainer contact is established, it should be added here before the first community release.
